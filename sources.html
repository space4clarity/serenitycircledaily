How we use these books.

Our posts offer original commentary rather than reproduce chapters, daily readings, or treatment programs. Short quotations are checked against the source text. PDF page numbers refer to the file’s page count; EPUB sections refer to the edition used.

We draw connections between authors without suggesting that they agree on everything. A book appearing here is not an endorsement by its author, publisher, or fellowship.
