ABOUT THE CIRCLE

A little space
for recovery.

For people navigating addiction recovery and sobriety, one ordinary day at a time.

One idea. No perfect version of you required.

Serenity Circle Daily brings together passages from recovery literature and books about mindfulness, self-compassion, and relationships. Each day offers an original reflection, a small optional invitation, and a question you can leave open.

The aim is thoughtful company, without shame, promises of transformation, or a prescribed path. Take what is useful and leave what does not fit your experience.

About the writing.

These reflections are generated and checked by AI using selected book passages. Quotations are verified against the source text, and an additional AI check reviews attribution, originality, and the boundary between reflection and treatment advice. Those checks can miss mistakes.

This is an independent reading project. It is not an official publication of AA, ACA, or any other fellowship. It offers reading and reflection, not professional care, diagnosis, or treatment instructions.
